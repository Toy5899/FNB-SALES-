
# FNB Sales Performance Case Study

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing FNB sales data to understand sales performance, profitability, pricing, customer demand, and the impact of promotional periods.

The project included data analysis, price elasticity analysis, KPI development, business insights, and the creation of interactive dashboards across multiple analytics platforms.

## Project Objectives

The main objectives were to:

- Analyse overall sales performance.
- Evaluate gross profit and profitability.
- Analyse quantity sold over time.
- Compare promotional periods with normal pricing periods.
- Evaluate the impact of promotions on customer demand.
- Calculate and analyse price elasticity.
- Identify sales and profitability trends.
- Develop interactive dashboards for business decision-making.

## Price Elasticity Analysis

Price elasticity was analysed to understand how changes in price affected quantity demanded during promotional periods.

| Promotion Period | Price Elasticity |
|------------------|-----------------:|
| Promotion 1 | -2.41 |
| Promotion 2 | -11.43 |
| Promotion 3 | -12.51 |

All three promotional periods recorded negative price elasticity, showing an inverse relationship between price and quantity demanded.

Promotions 2 and 3 showed a stronger quantity response to price changes compared with Promotion 1.

## Key Insights

- Promotional pricing had a noticeable impact on quantity sold.
- Promotions 2 and 3 showed the strongest demand response to changes in price.
- Sales and gross profit varied across the analysed period.
- Higher sales did not always translate into higher profitability.
- Promotional performance should be evaluated using both sales volume and profitability.

## Recommendations

Promotional strategies should balance increased customer demand with gross profit performance.

Price elasticity can be used to support future pricing decisions by identifying how strongly customers respond to changes in price.

Future promotional campaigns should consider sales volume, pricing, costs, and gross profit together rather than evaluating sales performance alone.
